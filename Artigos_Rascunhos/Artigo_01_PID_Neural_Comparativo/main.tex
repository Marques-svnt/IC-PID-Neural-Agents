\begin{abstract}
% TODO: Escrever abstract (150–250 palavras)
% Deve cobrir: problema, abordagem, resultados principais, contribuição
This paper presents a comparative analysis between classical PID tuning methods
(Ziegler–Nichols, Cohen–Coon, Internal Model Control) and a neural network-based 
adaptive PID controller orchestrated by an autonomous multi-agent system implemented 
with LangGraph. The controllers are evaluated on a simulated non-isothermal 
Continuous Stirred Tank Reactor (CSTR), a benchmark chemical process exhibiting 
strong nonlinearities due to Arrhenius kinetics. Performance metrics including 
Integral Absolute Error (IAE), Integral Time-weighted Absolute Error (ITAE), and 
Total Variation (TV) of the manipulated variable are used for quantitative comparison 
under setpoint tracking, load disturbance rejection, and parametric uncertainty scenarios.
\end{abstract}
