The non-isothermal CSTR with a first-order exothermic reaction $A \rightarrow B$ 
is described by the following system of Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs), 
derived from mass and energy balances:

\subsection{Concentration Balance}
\begin{equation}
    \frac{dC_A}{dt} = \frac{q}{V}(C_{Af} - C_A) - k_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{E}{RT}\right) C_A
    \label{eq:concentration}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Energy Balance}
\begin{equation}
    \frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{q}{V}(T_f - T) 
    + \frac{(-\Delta H)}{\rho C_p} k_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{E}{RT}\right) C_A 
    + \frac{UA}{V\rho C_p}(T_c - T)
    \label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}

where the controlled variable is the reactor temperature $T(t)$, the manipulated 
variable is the coolant jacket temperature $T_c(t)$, and the load disturbances 
are the feed concentration $C_{Af}$ and feed temperature $T_f$.
