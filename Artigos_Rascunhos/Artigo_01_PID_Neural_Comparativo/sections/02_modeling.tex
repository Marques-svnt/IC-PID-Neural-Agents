\subsection{Non-linear Physical Plant Model}
A continuous stirred tank reactor (CSTR) undergoing an irreversible, first-order exothermic reaction $A \xrightarrow{k} B$ is considered. To account for realistic heat exchange dynamics, the cooling jacket is modeled explicitly as a dynamic lumped system rather than assuming instantaneous jacket temperature control.

The non-linear system is governed by three coupled ordinary differential equations (ODEs) derived from dynamic mass and energy balances:

\begin{align}
    \frac{dC_A}{dt} &= \frac{q}{V}(C_{Af} - C_A) - r_A(C_A, T) \label{eq:cstr_ca} \\
    \frac{dT}{dt}   &= \frac{q}{V}(T_f - T) + \frac{(-\Delta H)}{\rho C_p} r_A(C_A, T) - \frac{UA}{V \rho C_p}(T - T_j) \label{eq:cstr_t} \\
    \frac{dT_j}{dt} &= \frac{q_j}{V_j}(T_{jf} - T_j) + \frac{UA}{V_j \rho_j C_{pj}}(T - T_j) \label{eq:cstr_tj}
\end{align}
where the reaction rate $r_A(C_A, T)$ follows Arrhenius temperature dependency:
\begin{equation}
    r_A(C_A, T) = k_0 \exp\left(-\frac{E}{R\,T}\right) C_A
    \label{eq:arrhenius}
\end{equation}

In this formulation, the state vector is $\mathbf{x} = [C_A, T, T_j]^T$, where $C_A$ is the reactant concentration in the reactor, $T$ is the internal reactor temperature, and $T_j$ is the cooling jacket temperature. The manipulated variable is the coolant volumetric flow rate $u(t) = q_j(t)$. The primary controlled variable is the reactor temperature $y(t) = T(t)$.

\subsection{Actuator Nonlinearity and Physical Saturation}
Industrial control valves cannot deliver infinite flow rates or instantaneously switch valve positions. Consequently, the manipulated input $u(t) = q_j(t)$ is subjected to hard amplitude saturation and rate-of-change (slew rate) limits:
\begin{align}
    u_{\min} &\le q_j(t) \le u_{\max} \\
    \left|\frac{dq_j}{dt}\right| &\le S_{\max}
\end{align}
where $u_{\min} = 0.0$~L/min (fully closed valve), $u_{\max} = 300.0$~L/min (maximum valve capacity), and $S_{\max} = 80.0$~L/min$^2$.

\subsection{Nominal Operating Parameters and Equilibrium}
The nominal physico-chemical parameters of the CSTR benchmark are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:cstr_parameters}. Under the nominal steady-state coolant flow rate $q_{j,ss} = 100.0$~L/min, the non-linear root-finding problem $\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{x}_{ss}, q_{j,ss}) = \mathbf{0}$ yields the nominal operating point:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{x}_{ss} = \begin{bmatrix} C_{A,ss} \\ T_{ss} \\ T_{j,ss} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0502\text{ mol/L} \\ 396.65\text{ K} \\ 348.33\text{ K} \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}
which corresponds to a high conversion regime ($X_A \approx 95\%$) where the reactor is highly sensitive to thermal perturbations.
